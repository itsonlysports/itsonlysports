I write as **Low Owned**. In daily fantasy, "low owned" means the player almost nobody else picked. That is the whole idea here: the best play is often the one the crowd is ignoring.

This blog looks at sports and DFS through a leverage lens. I care less about who the best player is and more about where the field is wrong. I won't be right every time. Right half the time, in the right spots, is worth something.

## What you'll find here

- **The Leverage Play:** the weekly edge, before the slate locks.
- **Enjoy It Monday:** the best moments and worst beats from the weekend.
- **Receipts:** how my picks and lineups actually did, wins and losses.
- **Previews:** majors, playoffs and the big events.

## The fine print

I keep score in public on the [Receipts](/receipts/) page. If I ever earn money from a link, the post will say so. More on that in the [Disclosures](/disclosures/).

It's only sports. Enjoy it.
